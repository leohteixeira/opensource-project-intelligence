# ADR-006: Present Health as Auditable Dimensions With an Optional Overall Score

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-08-20

## Context

The product must answer whether a project appears healthy without equating popularity with health or
hiding different forms of risk inside one label. Independent dimensions communicate activity,
community, maintenance, stability, security, and adoption separately. Some users still benefit from
a compact overall signal when screening several technologies.

## Decision

The primary health presentation will consist of independent deterministic dimensions. An overall
health score may be shown as a secondary, optional summary, never as the sole or dominant health
result. Every dimension and overall score must expose its formula or model version, observation
window, contributing factors, source evidence, and missing-data treatment.

The product will not label a project simply `healthy` or `unhealthy`. Unknown and not-applicable
inputs must remain distinguishable from poor performance, and a score must not be published when its
declared evidence requirements are not satisfied.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Dimensions Only

- **Description**: Show separate health dimensions without any aggregate.
- **Pros**: Preserves nuance and avoids false precision.
- **Cons**: Makes rapid portfolio screening and high-level comparison more laborious.
- **Why rejected**: The selected direction permits a transparent secondary summary.

### Alternative 2: Overall Score as the Primary Health Signal

- **Description**: Lead with one aggregate score and provide dimensions as drill-down details.
- **Pros**: Fast to scan and easy to rank.
- **Cons**: Encourages users to ignore materially different risk profiles and missing evidence.
- **Why rejected**: It conflicts with the requirement to make independent health dimensions primary.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Users can identify the specific strengths and risks behind a health assessment.
- Comparisons remain useful without reducing every project to one opaque rank.
- Formula and evidence visibility make health calculations reproducible.

### Negative

- The interface must communicate several dimensions, provenance, and missing data without overload.
- Formula changes require score versioning and recalculation rules.

### Risks

- Users may still overvalue the optional aggregate; UI hierarchy and explanatory copy must keep it
  subordinate to the dimensions.
- Cross-source coverage can differ; comparisons must disclose which dimensions are actually
  comparable.

## Implementation Notes

The precise dimension formulas, default weights, evidence thresholds, and presentation hierarchy
will be frozen during Stage 2 surface and technical design.
