# ADR-025: Always Show the Overall Health Score When Calculable

## Status

Accepted; clarifies the visibility rule in ADR-006

## Date

2026-08-21

## Context

ADR-006 made independent health dimensions primary and allowed a transparent secondary overall
score. The browser surface still needed to decide whether a valid calculated score appears
automatically, requires an Admin display toggle, or remains hidden.

## Decision

Always show the overall health score as a secondary summary whenever the active definition's
declared evidence requirements are met. Never render a score when it cannot be calculated. The seven
independent dimensions remain primary, and the score exposes its version, weights, factors, window,
cutoff, evidence, and missing-data handling.

## Alternatives Considered

### Alternative 1: Admin-controlled visibility

- **Description**: Hide the score unless an Admin enables it.
- **Pros**: Gives governance explicit control over whether users see aggregation.
- **Cons**: Makes a valid result inconsistently discoverable across workspaces or policy changes.
- **Why rejected**: The selected surface always exposes a calculable result.

### Alternative 2: Never display the score

- **Description**: Keep only independent dimensions.
- **Pros**: Avoids aggregation pressure.
- **Cons**: Removes the approved secondary summary entirely.
- **Why rejected**: A transparent aggregate remains part of the product.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Users see a consistent summary when sufficient evidence exists.
- Missing evidence cannot create a misleading partial score.

### Negative

- Users may overweight the aggregate despite its secondary placement.

### Risks

- Visual prominence could turn the score into a binary verdict; design and copy must keep dimensions
  primary and never label a Project healthy or unhealthy.

## Implementation Notes

The overall-score definition remains versioned and closed like the metric catalog.
